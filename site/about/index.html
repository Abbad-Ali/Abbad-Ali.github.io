About

Abbad Ali.

I work with Professor Stephen H. Shore on the economics of AI safety at Georgia State University. I’m interested in how incentives and policy can help us make better decisions about emerging technologies.

My path here has taken me from economics and mathematics at LUMS to public-sector consulting in Pakistan, and then to graduate study in financial mathematics and quantitative risk at Georgia State. Working on public healthcare helped me see how much depends on the gap between a model and the decisions people can actually make.

Outside research, I write essays and am working on two books about attention and life online.
