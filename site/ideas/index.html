    Flygrad

    Study-abroad guidance for students who cannot afford a consultant.

    5students I meant to help
    →
    5,000+students who applied

    I decided to help five students with their plans to study abroad. Then a reel went viral, and more than 5,000 students applied.

    That was the start of Flygrad. I built an application to make the guidance easier to reach. The aim is simple: the chance to study abroad should depend less on whether you can pay for advice.
