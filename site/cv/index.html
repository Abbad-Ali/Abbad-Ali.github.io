Market-informed identification and regulation of AI risk

Work in progress

With Stephen H. Shore.

Research on how prices of feasible contracts can help identify risky AI activities and the firms engaged in them. The framework combines market prices with firm-activity information to estimate firm-specific probabilities of large, non-catastrophic losses under an explicit homogeneity assumption.

Beliefs and values about existential risk

Work in progress

With Stephen H. Shore, Ivy Diallo, Kae Gruner.

An ongoing pre/post survey study of selection into existential-risk coursework and changes in beliefs, values, and risk perception, using survey instruments, treatment and comparison groups, regression, and sensitivity analysis.
