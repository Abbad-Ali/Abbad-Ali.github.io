I helped develop the online version of this course and contributed to selected modules, primarily on artificial intelligence and humanity’s future. I subsequently prepared the free public edition.

The course brings probability, decision theory, welfare economics, and quantitative modeling to questions about existential risk. It is based on Professor Stephen H. Shore’s course at Georgia State University.

The materials are freely available online. The course’s contributor page describes my role.
