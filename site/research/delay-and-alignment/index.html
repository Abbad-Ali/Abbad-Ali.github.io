From January to June 2026, I worked as a Research Assistant to Professor Stephen H. Shore and Professor Richard Peter at Georgia State University.

I conducted a systematic literature review across decision theory, existential risk, and AI governance to support the paper’s theoretical framework.

My role was research assistance. Professor Shore’s research page lists the working paper, by Shore and Peter, under the current title Managing Existential Risk from AI: Investing in Delay, Learning, and Alignment.
