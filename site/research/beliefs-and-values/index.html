Beliefs and Values About Existential Risk

A pre/post survey study of selection into existential-risk coursework and its effects on beliefs, values, and risk perception.

June 2026–present

  • Existential risk
  • Education

Collaborators

Abbad Ali, Stephen H. Shore, Ivy Diallo, Kae Gruner

My contribution

Study design, identification strategy, survey instruments, and analysis.

Materials

  • Research overview · Stephen H. Shore ↗

Who chooses to study existential risk, and does the coursework change how students think about it?

With Stephen H. Shore, Ivy Diallo, and Kae Gruner, I design and run a pre/post survey study with treatment and control groups. We study selection into the course and its causal effects on beliefs, values, and risk perception.

My work includes specifying the identification strategy, building the survey instruments, and analyzing responses using regression and sensitivity analysis.

The work is ongoing: I am developing the study, collecting responses, and analyzing the data as they arrive.

The study asks how to teach material that requires judgment about catastrophic risk while preserving room for legitimate disagreement.
