  2. Book in progressDraft excerpt

    Offline Is a Personality Now

    The hollow one. The one that shows up at around 11pm on a Tuesday when you have been scrolling for two hours and nothing was that interesting and you are not really tired and you are not really awake and you do not know what you want but you know it is not this.

    And yet. And yet you keep going.

    Read a little more

    This book wants something harder than that.

    It wants you to ask one question, seriously, maybe for the first time: who are you when no one is watching?

    Not the version of you with the good lighting. Not the one who types “lol” when nothing is funny. Not the one who has an opinion about everything the feed told you to have an opinion about today. The one underneath.
