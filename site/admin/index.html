In the editor

Profile, books & projects

About & profile holds your introduction, experience, teaching, recognition, and links. The other sections hold your books, research, consulting cases, and ideas.

New pages

Create a page under New pages. Keep Draft on while writing. Once it is ready, switch Draft off and add its address under Tabs & navigation. A page called my-page uses pages/my-page/.

Menu tabs

Rename, reorder, add, or remove tabs in Tabs & navigation. Switching off Visible hides a tab; it does not delete its page. To remove a custom page from the website, switch Draft on or delete it.

Article preferences

Writing preferences lets you pin Substack articles or hide individual links. Put pinned links in the order you want them shown. All remaining articles appear newest first.

Substack articles refresh daily. Editing takes place in Pages CMS and requires GitHub access.
