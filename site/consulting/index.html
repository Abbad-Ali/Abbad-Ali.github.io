Health systems

A roadmap is only the start.

My contribution

03

Public health insurance

Access has to be affordable to last.

How can public insurance control costs while preserving access to essential care?

My contribution

Worked on the financial sustainability of Punjab’s Sehat Sahulat program, including cost-containment and risk-based pricing. Connected coverage and benefit choices to their fiscal implications.

The output

Analysis and policy recommendations on coverage, benefits, and financial sustainability.

Program fiscal burden

$304 million→$161 million

A program-level change, achieved through a wider team effort.

  • Risk-based pricing
  • Cost containment
  • Benefit design
